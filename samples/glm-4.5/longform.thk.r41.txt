四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 - observing the patient's appearance, complexion, tongue, body shape, spirit (神), etc.
2. 闻诊 - listening to sounds (voice, breathing, coughing) and smelling odors
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 - pulse diagnosis (脉诊) and palpation (按诊)

合参 means combining and synthesizing all the information from these four methods to make a comprehensive diagnosis.

Key points to cover:
- The origin: This concept comes from classic TCM texts, particularly 《黄帝内经》 and 《难经》. The famous saying "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧" from 《难经》
- Each of the four methods briefly explained
- Why combining them is important: each method has limitations; only by integrating all information can one get a complete picture (辨证论治 basis)
- The principle: 中医强调"四诊合参"，不可偏废 - one shouldn't rely on just one method
- The purpose: to conduct 辨证 for treatment
