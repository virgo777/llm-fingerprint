1. 望诊  - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape, tongue, etc.

3. 问诊  - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊  - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse and palpating the body
