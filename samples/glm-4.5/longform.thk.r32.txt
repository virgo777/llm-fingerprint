四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 - observing the patient's complexion, body shape, tongue, spirit/vitality, etc.
2. 闻诊 - listening to sounds (voice, breathing, coughing) and smelling odors
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 - pulse diagnosis and palpation

合参 means combining/synthesizing all these methods together.

The concept of 四诊合参 emphasizes that these four methods should not be used in isolation but should be combined and cross-verified to form a comprehensive understanding of the patient's condition. This is a fundamental principle in TCM diagnostics.

Historical origins:
- The concept dates back to ancient times. 《黄帝内经》 laid the foundation
- 扁鹊 is traditionally credited with establishing these methods - "望闻问切" 
- 《难经》 stated: "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧" (One who knows by looking is called divine; by listening, a sage; by asking, skilled; by pulse-taking, clever)

Key points about 四诊合参:
1. Each method has its own scope and limitations
2. They must be integrated because diseases are complex
3. The information gathered must be comprehensive
4. It's the foundation for 辨证论治
5. If there are contradictions between different diagnostic findings, careful analysis is needed (舍脉从症 or 舍症从脉 - sometimes disregarding pulse in favor of symptoms, or vice versa)

The "四诊合参" principle emphasizes:
- 望诊: observing 神, 色, 形, 态, tongue
- 闻诊: listening to voice, breathing; smelling odors
- 问诊: the "十问" (ten questions) about symptoms, history
- 切诊: pulse-taking (脉诊) and palpation (按诊)

The purpose is to gather comprehensive clinical information for syndrome differentiation (辨证).

Why integration is needed:
- Disease manifestations are complex and varied
- A single method may be insufficient or misleading
- Different methods examine different aspects
- Only by combining them can an accurate diagnosis be made
