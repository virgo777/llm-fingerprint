四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊  - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), color (色), form (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊  - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊  - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊  - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

The concept of 四诊合参 emphasizes that these four methods should be used together, not in isolation. Each method provides different information, and only by combining all four can a physician make an accurate diagnosis (辨证).
