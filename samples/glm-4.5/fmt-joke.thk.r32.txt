The wife buying baozi joke:
程序员的妻子对他说："下班回来的路上买十个包子，如果看到卖西瓜的，就买一个。"
程序员回到家，手里只拿着一个包子。

This is a great one because it plays on the conditional logic - "如果看到卖西瓜的，就买一个" - the programmer interprets "买一个" as replacing the original instruction rather than adding to it, which is how programmers often interpret ambiguous instructions (if condition X, then do Y instead of Z, rather than in addition to Z).
