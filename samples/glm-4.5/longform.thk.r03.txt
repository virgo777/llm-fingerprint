四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in Traditional Chinese Medicine:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors from the body or secretions
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Palpation: pulse diagnosis (脉诊/切脉) and palpation of the body

合参 means combining/synthesizing all four methods together for comprehensive analysis.

The concept emphasizes that these four methods should not be used in isolation but should be combined and cross-verified to form a complete understanding of the patient's condition. This is fundamental to 辨证论治 (pattern differentiation and treatment).

The origin: The four diagnostic methods were established by 扁鹊 (Bian Que) traditionally, and systematized in 《难经》 and later by 张仲景 in 《伤寒杂病论》. The term 四诊 and the emphasis on combining them appears in classical texts.
