四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in Traditional Chinese Medicine:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation - observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling - listening to voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation - feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

合参 means combining and synthesizing all four methods' findings together to form a comprehensive diagnosis. The key principle is that no single method should be used in isolation - the practitioner must integrate all information (四诊合参) to make an accurate diagnosis (辨证).
