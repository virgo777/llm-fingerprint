四诊合参 refers to the comprehensive use of four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

The concept of 合参 means combining all four methods together for comprehensive analysis - not relying on just one method. The idea is that each method provides different information, and only by integrating all of them can a practitioner accurately determine the pattern/syndrome (辨证) and make a correct diagnosis.

Key points to cover:
- Definition: 四诊 refers to 望、闻、问、切 four diagnostic methods
- The principle originated from classical texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was elaborated in 《难经》, and later physicians emphasized the importance of combining all four
- Each method's content:
