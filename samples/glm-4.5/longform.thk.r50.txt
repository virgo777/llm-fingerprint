四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in Traditional Chinese Medicine:
1. 望诊 - observing the patient's complexion, body shape, tongue, spirit/vitality, etc.
2. 闻诊 - includes both listening to sounds (voice, breathing, coughing) and smelling odors (body odor, breath)
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. The classic "十问" (ten questions)
4. 切诊 - includes pulse diagnosis (脉诊) and palpation (按诊) of the body

合参 means combining/synthesizing these four methods together for comprehensive analysis.

Key points to cover:
1. Definition: 四诊合参 is the fundamental diagnostic principle in TCM where the four diagnostic methods are used together, not in isolation
2. The four methods individually:
   - 望诊: observing spirit (神), color (色), form (形), state (态), tongue (舌象) - "望而知之谓之神"
   - 闻诊: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors - "闻而知之谓之圣"
   - 问诊: the most detailed method, asking about symptoms, history - "问而知之谓之工"
   - 切诊: pulse taking and palpation - "切而知之谓之巧"
3. Why they must be combined: each method has limitations; only by integrating all information can a comprehensive understanding of the patient's condition be achieved
4. Theoretical basis: based on 整体观念, the human body is an organic whole, and external manifestations reflect internal conditions (司外揣内, 见微知著)
5. Clinical significance: prevents misdiagnosis, ensures accuracy of 辨证论治
6. Classic quote: 《难经》has "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧"
