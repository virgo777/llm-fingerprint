四诊合参 refers to the comprehensive use of the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 - observing the patient's appearance, complexion, tongue, body shape, spirit/vitality (神), etc.
2. 闻诊 - listening to sounds (voice, breathing, coughing) and smelling odors
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问歌)
4. 切诊 - includes pulse diagnosis (脉诊) and palpation (按诊)

四诊合参 means that these four methods should be used together comprehensively, not relying on just one. The concept emphasizes:
- Each diagnostic method has its own scope and limitations
- They must be combined to get a complete picture
- This is the foundation for 辨证论治
- The idea comes from classic texts like 《黄帝内经》 and 《难经》
