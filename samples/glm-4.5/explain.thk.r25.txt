BIA (Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, and body water, by measuring the resistance (impedance) of electrical currents as they pass through body tissues.

Key points about BIA:
- It works by sending a safe, low-level electrical current through the body
- Different tissues (water, muscle, fat) have different electrical conductivity
- Water and muscle (which contain water and electrolytes) conduct electricity well (low impedance)
- Fat resists electricity more (high impedance)
- Based on impedance measurements, formulas are used to estimate body composition (body fat percentage, lean body mass, total body water, etc.)
- It's non-invasive, quick, relatively inexpensive
- Commonly used in fitness centers, hospitals, and health assessments
- Accuracy can be affected by hydration status, food intake, exercise, etc.
