四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), color (色), shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

合参 means combining all these methods together for comprehensive analysis. The key principle is that no single diagnostic method should be used in isolation—doctors must integrate information from all four methods to form a complete understanding of the patient's condition (证候/证), which then guides treatment (辨证论治).
