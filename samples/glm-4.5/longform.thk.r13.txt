1. 望诊  - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), color/complexion (色), form (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.

3. 问诊  - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)

The concept of 四诊合参 emphasizes that these four methods should be used together comprehensively, not in isolation. The doctor must integrate all the information gathered from these four methods to make an accurate diagnosis (辨证 - syndrome differentiation).
