BIA (Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, and body water content. It works by passing a small, safe electrical current through the body and measuring the impedance (resistance) to the flow of this current. Different tissues in the body have different water content and thus different electrical conductivities. For example, muscle tissue, which contains a lot of water and electrolytes, conducts electricity well (low impedance), while fat tissue contains less water and conducts electricity poorly (high impedance). By analyzing these measurements, BIA can estimate body composition.
