The Four Great Classical Novels (四大名著) of Chinese literature are:

1. 《三国演义》 - 罗贯中
2. 《水浒传》 - 施耐庵
3. 《西游记》 - 吴承恩
4. 《红楼梦》 - 曹雪芹 (Cao Xueqin, with the last 40 chapters often attributed to 高鹗 Gao E)

Some notes:
- 《水浒传》authorship is sometimes debated between 施耐庵 and 罗贯中, but the traditional attribution is Shi Nai'an
- 《红楼梦》- Cao Xueqin wrote the first 80 chapters; Gao E (and/or Cheng Weiyuan) is traditionally credited with compiling/editing the last 40 chapters
- 《西游记》- Wu Cheng'en is the traditional attribution
