四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 - observing the patient's appearance, complexion, tongue, body shape, spirit (神), etc.
2. 闻诊 - listening to sounds (voice, breathing, coughing) and smelling odors
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 - feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

合参 means combining/synthesizing the information from all four methods to make a comprehensive diagnosis.

The concept emphasizes:
- Each method has its own focus and limitations
- The four methods should be used together, not in isolation
- This reflects the holistic view of TCM (整体观念)
- It originated from classic texts like 《黄帝内经》and was systematized in 《难经》, with the famous saying "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧" (from 《难经·六十一难》)
- 张仲景 in 《伤寒论》 also emphasized combining methods
