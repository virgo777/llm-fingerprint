Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis (BIA) is a technique for measuring body composition. It uses the different conductive properties of water-rich tissues vs fat tissue. A weak alternating current is passed through the body, and impedance is measured. Since muscle contains lots of water and electrolytes (low impedance) and fat contains little water (high impedance), BIA can estimate body fat percentage, muscle mass, body water, etc. It's non-invasive, quick, cheap, commonly used in body composition scales and clinical nutrition assessment.
