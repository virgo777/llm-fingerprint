四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

合参 means combining/comprehensively analyzing these four methods together.

Key points about 四诊合参:
- It's the fundamental diagnostic approach in TCM
- Each of the four methods provides unique information
- No single method should be used in isolation
- The four methods must be combined and analyzed comprehensively to understand the disease pattern (辨证)
- This reflects the holistic view (整体观念) of TCM
- It's associated with 辨证论治 (pattern differentiation and treatment determination)
- Historical origins: attributed to 扁鹊 (Bian Que), systematized in classic texts like 《黄帝内经》, detailed in 《难经》which states "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧"
