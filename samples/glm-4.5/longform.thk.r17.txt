四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), form (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

四诊合参 means that a physician must combine and synthesize all four diagnostic methods' findings to make a comprehensive analysis and accurate diagnosis (辨证), rather than relying on just one method.

The concept comes from classic texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was emphasized in 《难经》: "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧" (comparing the levels of skill).
