四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. The classic "十问" (ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

合参 means combining/synthesizing all four methods together for comprehensive analysis. The key principle is that no single diagnostic method should be used in isolation - the physician must integrate all four types of information to form a complete picture (证, syndrome/pattern) before making a diagnosis and treatment plan.

Historical origins: The four diagnostic methods were systematized by 扁鹊 (Bian Que) according to legend, and formally documented in 《难经》and later in 《千金要方》by 孙思邈. The phrase "四诊合参" emphasizes that these methods must be used together.
