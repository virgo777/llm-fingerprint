四诊合参 refers to the TCM diagnostic principle of combining and cross-referencing four diagnostic methods:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), form (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

The concept of 四诊合参 emphasizes:
- These four methods should be used together, not in isolation
- Each method provides different information about the patient's condition
- Only by combining all four can a practitioner make an accurate 辨证 (syndrome differentiation)
- Originated from classical texts - 《黄帝内经》 laid foundations, and 扁鹊 (Bian Que) is traditionally associated with the four diagnostics. The term and systematic description appears in 《难经》 which mentions 望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧
- 明代 张景岳 in 《景岳全书》 discussed the "十问歌"
- The phrase "四诊合参" itself emphasizes comprehensive analysis

The principle: 单一诊法有局限性，必须综合运用才能全面了解病情，正确辨证论治。
