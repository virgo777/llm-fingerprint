Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis (BIA) is a technique for assessing human body composition. It works by applying a safe micro-current to the human body, and because different tissues (such as muscle, fat, and water) have different electrical conductivity properties—water and muscle-rich tissues conduct electricity well, while fat conducts electricity poorly—by measuring the body's impedance value, combined with parameters such as height, weight, age, and gender, algorithms are used to estimate body composition indicators such as body fat rate, muscle mass, and body water content. This method is non-invasive, rapid, low-cost, and is widely used in health check-ups, fitness assessments, and clinical nutrition monitoring.
