四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

四诊合参 emphasizes that these four methods should be used together, not in isolation, to comprehensively understand the patient's condition. This is the principle of "诸诊合参" or combining all diagnostic information to make an accurate syndrome differentiation (辨证).

The concept originates from classic texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was further developed in 《难经》. The famous saying "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧" comes from 《难经》.
