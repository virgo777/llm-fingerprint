四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse and palpating the body

The concept of 四诊合参 emphasizes that these four methods should be used together comprehensively, not in isolation, to form a complete understanding of the patient's condition for correct syndrome differentiation (辨证) and treatment (论治).
