Some notes:
- 《三国演义》: Romance of the Three Kingdoms, attributed to Luo Guanzhong (罗贯中), written in the 14th century during the late Yuan/early Ming dynasty
- 《水浒传》: Water Margin, attributed to Shi Nai'an (施耐庵), also 14th century. Some editions credit both Shi Nai'an and Luo Guanzhong
- 《西游记》: Journey to the West, attributed to Wu Cheng'en (吴承恩), 16th century Ming dynasty
- 《红楼梦》: Dream of the Red Chamber / A Dream of Red Mansions, by Cao Xueqin (曹雪芹), 18th century Qing dynasty. The last 40 chapters are often attributed to Gao E (高鹗) who completed/edited it
