The four diagnostic methods:

望诊 (Inspection): Observing the patient's spirit (神), color/complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc. "望而知之谓之神"

闻诊 (Listening and Smelling): Listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors from the body, breath, etc.

问诊 (Inquiry): Asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, pain location, sleep, appetite, bowel/bladder function, etc. "问而知之谓之工"

切诊 (Pulse-taking and Palpation): Feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊). Pulse diagnosis involves examining the pulse at the wrist (寸口脉), with various pulse qualities like 浮、沉、迟、数 etc.

四诊合参 means that doctors should not rely on a single diagnostic method but combine all four to comprehensively analyze and determine the condition. This is to avoid misdiagnosis. The concept emphasizes holistic view (整体观念) and dialectical analysis (辨证论治).

Historical origins: The four diagnostic methods were systematically established in 《黄帝内经》, and 扁鹊 is often credited as a master of diagnosis. The term "四诊" was popularized later. 张仲景 in 《伤寒杂病论》 emphasized 四诊合参.
