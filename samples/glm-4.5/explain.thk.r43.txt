Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis (BIA) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, body water, etc. 

Key principles:
1. BIA works by passing a small, safe electrical current through the body
2. Different tissues have different electrical conductivity - water and muscle (which contain water and electrolytes) conduct electricity well (low impedance), while fat tissue resists electrical flow (high impedance)
3. By measuring the impedance (resistance) to the electrical current, the device can estimate body composition

Applications:
- Body fat percentage measurement
- Muscle mass estimation
- Total body water assessment
- Used in fitness centers, hospitals, health checkups

Advantages:
- Non-invasive
- Quick
- Relatively inexpensive
- No radiation

Limitations:
- Accuracy can be affected by hydration status, food intake, exercise, etc.
