BIA (生物电阻抗分析) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, and body water. It works by passing a small, safe electrical current through the body and measuring the impedance (resistance) to the flow of that current.

The basic principle is:
- Water and muscle (which contain water and electrolytes) conduct electricity well (low impedance)
- Fat tissue conducts electricity poorly (high impedance)
- By measuring the impedance, the device can estimate the proportions of different body components
