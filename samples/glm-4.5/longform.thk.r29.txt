四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wang Zhen) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌诊 - tongue coating, tongue color, tongue shape)
2. 闻诊 (Wen Zhen) - Listening and Smelling: listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wen Zhen) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. The classic "十问" (ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qie Zhen) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

合参 means combining/synthesizing all four methods together - not relying on just one method but integrating all information to make a diagnosis (辨证).
