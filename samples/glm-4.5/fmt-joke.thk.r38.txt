Classic programmer jokes in Chinese:

1. 程序员最讨厌的四件事：写注释、写文档、别人不写注释、别人不写文档。
(Programmers hate four things: writing comments, writing documentation, others not writing comments, others not writing documentation.)

2. 为什么程序员总是分不清万圣节和圣诞节？因为 Oct 31 == Dec 25（八进制的31等于十进制的25）
(Why can't programmers tell Halloween and Christmas apart? Because Oct 31 == Dec 25 — Octal 31 equals Decimal 25!)

3. 一个程序员去买菜，老婆说："买一斤包子，如果看到卖西瓜的，买一个。" 程序员回来时手里拿着一个包子。老婆问为什么，他说："因为我看到了卖西瓜的。"
(A programmer goes shopping. His wife says: "Buy a pound of buns, and if you see a watermelon seller, buy one." The programmer comes back with one bun. His wife asks why, he says: "Because I saw a watermelon seller.")

This is a classic joke about programmers interpreting instructions literally like code logic.

4. 程序员相亲，姑娘问他："你有房吗？" 他说："有啊，GitHub上有很多。"

5. 两个程序员在路上走，一个说："我老婆昨天生了个儿子。" 另一个问："恭喜！是头胎吗？" 第一个回答："不，是v2.0。"

6. 程序员的妻子让他去超市："买一斤苹果，如果看到有卖鸡蛋的，买十个。" 程序员回来买了十个苹果。妻子问为什么，他说："因为超市有卖鸡蛋的。"

7. 为什么程序员喜欢暗色模式？因为光会吸引bug（bugs - 虫子）。
(Why do programmers like dark mode? Because light attracts bugs!)

8. 程序员的世界里只有10种人：懂二进制的和不懂的。
(There are 10 types of people in the world: those who understand binary and those who don't.)

In programming logic, the conditional overrode the original quantity — like a variable being reassigned.
