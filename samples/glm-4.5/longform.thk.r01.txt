四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

四诊合参 means that doctors must combine and analyze all four diagnostic methods together rather than relying on just one, to make an accurate diagnosis (辨证 - syndrome differentiation).

The concept originates from classic texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was emphasized by 张仲景 in 《伤寒杂病论》.

Key points:
- 望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧 (from 《难经》)
- The four methods each have their own unique value and limitations
- Only by combining all four can one avoid misdiagnosis
- 舌诊 and 脉诊 are particularly important
- This is the foundation for 辨证论治
