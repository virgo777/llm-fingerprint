四诊 (Four Diagnostic Methods):
1. 望 (Wàng) - Inspection/Observation: Looking at the patient's complexion, body shape, tongue, spirit (神), etc. Includes 舌诊 (tongue diagnosis)
2. 闻 (Wén) - Listening and Smelling: Listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问 (Wèn) - Inquiry/Questioning: Asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. The classic "十问" (ten questions)
4. 切 (Qiè) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: Feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

合参 (Combined Analysis): The four methods should be used together, integrating all information obtained to make a comprehensive diagnosis (辨证). No single method should be relied upon exclusively.

- The principle of "四诊合参" emphasizes holistic view (整体观念) and 辨证论治
