Key points:
- BIA = Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis, bioelectrical impedance analysis
- Principle: different body tissues (fat, muscle, water) conduct electrical current differently; lean tissue with high water content conducts well, fat conducts poorly
- Method: low-level electrical current passed through body, measure impedance/resistance
- Estimates body composition: body fat percentage, muscle mass, total body water, etc.
- Applications: health assessment, nutrition monitoring, fitness
- Advantages: non-invasive, quick, inexpensive
