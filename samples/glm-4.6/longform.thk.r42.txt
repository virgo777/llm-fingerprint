四诊合参 refers to the comprehensive analysis of the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

The concept of 合参 means combining all four methods together for comprehensive analysis, rather than relying on just one method. This is because each method has limitations, and only by integrating all information can a doctor make an accurate 辨证 (syndrome differentiation) and diagnosis.

Key principles:
- Each of the four methods provides different information
- They must be used together, not in isolation
- "四诊合参" prevents misdiagnosis
- It's the foundation of 辨证论治 (syndrome differentiation and treatment)
- Historical origin: mentioned in classics like 《黄帝内经》, and the concept was emphasized by 张仲景 in 《伤寒杂病论》

There's a famous saying: "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧" from 《难经》.

The principle against relying on one method: "切脉动数，按尺部而不得" or the warning in 《伤寒论》 where 张仲景 criticized doctors who only rely on pulse-taking: "省疾问病，务在口给，相对斯须，便处汤药，按寸不及尺，握手不及足..."
