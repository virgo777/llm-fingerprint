Let me go with the bun joke as the main one, it's genuinely funny and works well:

老婆给程序员老公打电话："下班顺路买十个包子，如果看到卖西瓜的，买一个。"
程序员回家，手里只拿着一个包子。
老婆问："怎么只买了一个包子？"
程序员："因为我看到卖西瓜的了。"

This is a classic joke about how programmers interpret conditional statements literally - "if you see watermelon seller, buy one" - the programmer interprets "buy one" as replacing "buy ten buns" rather than adding one watermelon.
