2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling - listening to the patient's voice, breathing, and smelling odors

The concept of "合参" means combining all four methods together for comprehensive analysis, rather than relying on just one method. This is a fundamental principle in TCM diagnosis.
