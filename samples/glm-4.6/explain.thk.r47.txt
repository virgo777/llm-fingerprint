BIA (Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, body water, etc. It works by passing a small, safe electrical current through the body and measuring the impedance (resistance) to the flow of this current. Since different tissues (fat, muscle, water) have different electrical conductivity, the impedance measurements can be used to estimate the proportions of various body components.
