四诊合参 refers to the comprehensive analysis using the four diagnostic methods of TCM:

1. 望诊 - observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 - listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 - pulse diagnosis (脉诊) and palpation (按诊)

The key concept is "合参" - combining all four methods for comprehensive analysis, because each method has limitations, and only by combining them can a doctor accurately determine the pattern (证候) and make a correct diagnosis.

Key principles:
- The four methods should not be used in isolation
- They complement each other
- 详见 早日 - "欲知其内者，当以观其外" type philosophy
- Related sayings: "四诊合参" emphasizes that doctors must not rely on just one method

Historical context: The four diagnostic methods were established in the Huangdi Neijing (黄帝内经) and systematized by Bian Que (扁鹊) according to tradition. The phrase "四诊" is often attributed to the systematic development through history.
