BIA (生物电阻抗分析) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, body water, etc. It works by passing a small, safe electrical current through the body and measuring the impedance (resistance) to the flow of this current. Different tissues in the body have different water content and thus different electrical conductivity. For example:
- Lean tissue (muscle) contains a lot of water and electrolytes, so it conducts electricity well (low impedance)
- Fat tissue contains less water, so it conducts electricity poorly (high impedance)

By measuring the impedance and using equations that take into account height, weight, age, and sex, the device can estimate:
- Body fat percentage
- Muscle mass (skeletal muscle mass)
- Total body water
- Basal metabolic rate
- etc.
