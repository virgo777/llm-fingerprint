The Four Diagnostic Methods (四诊) are:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape, tongue, etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse and palpating the body

四诊合参 means combining and synthesizing all four diagnostic methods to form a comprehensive understanding of the patient's condition. It emphasizes:
- No single method should be used in isolation
- The information from all four methods should be cross-verified and integrated
- This forms the basis for 辨证论治 (syndrome differentiation and treatment)
- It reflects the holistic view of TCM

The concept comes from classical texts - 《难经》states "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧" (He who knows by inspection is called divine; by listening, sagacious; by inquiry, skilled; by pulse-taking, adroit).

Historical origin: The term 四诊 appears in TCM classics. 《黄帝内经》laid the foundation. 张仲景 in 《伤寒杂病论》emphasized "观其脉证，知犯何逆，随证治之".

Key principles of 四诊合参:
1. 四诊并重 - All four methods are equally important
2. 相互印证 - Mutual verification of findings
3. 综合分析 - Comprehensive analysis
