四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), color/complexion (色), form (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, coughing; smelling odors of the body
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

合参 means combining/synthesizing all four methods together for comprehensive analysis.

Key points about 四诊合参:
- It's the fundamental diagnostic principle in TCM
- The four methods each have their own scope and limitations; no single method is sufficient
- Only by combining information from all four can a doctor make accurate 辨证 (syndrome differentiation)
- There's a saying: "四诊合参" emphasizes that doctors should not rely on just one method
- Related to the concept from 《难经》: "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧" (knowing by inspection is called divine, by listening is called sage-like, by asking is called skillful, by pulse-taking is called clever)
