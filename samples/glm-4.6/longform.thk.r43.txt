四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods of TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's complexion, tongue, body shape, spirit (神), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse and palpating the body

四诊合参 means combining all four methods comprehensively rather than relying on any single one. The concept emphasizes that these four methods must be used together, cross-verified, to accurately determine the syndrome (辨证).

四诊合参是中医"辨证论治"体系的基础。医生通过合参得出"证"的判断，才能确立治则治法、处方用药。这一原则体现了中医整体观念，强调从整体上认识疾病，是中医临床思维的核心。
