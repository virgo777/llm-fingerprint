四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation - observing the patient's complexion, body shape, tongue, spirit (神), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling - listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation - feeling the pulse and palpating the body

The concept of 合参 means combining all four methods together for comprehensive analysis, rather than relying on just one method. This ensures a more accurate diagnosis (辨证 - syndrome differentiation).
