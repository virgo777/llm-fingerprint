2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling - listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors

合参 means combining/comprehensively analyzing these four methods together to form a complete diagnosis.

- Each method has its own focus and limitations
- No single method alone can provide a complete picture
- The four methods must be used together, cross-referencing information
- This reflects the holistic thinking (整体观念) of TCM
- 辨证论治 (syndrome differentiation and treatment) depends on this comprehensive analysis
