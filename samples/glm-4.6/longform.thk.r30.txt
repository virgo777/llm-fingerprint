四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 - observing the patient's appearance, complexion, tongue, spirit (神), body shape, etc.
2. 闻诊 - listening to sounds like voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 - pulse diagnosis (脉诊) and palpation (按诊)

合参 means combining and comprehensively analyzing all four methods together.

Key points to cover:
1. Definition and origin - 四诊合参 comes from TCM diagnostic theory, with roots in 《黄帝内经》 and systematically developed in 《难经》, 《伤寒论》 etc. The term emphasizes that diagnosis should not rely on a single method.

2. The four methods:
- 望诊: observe 神, 色, 形, 态, tongue (舌诊 is very important - 舌质 and 舌苔)
- 闻诊: listen to voice, breathing, coughing; smell body odors, breath odor
- 问诊: the "十问" (ten questions) - about symptoms, onset, history, etc.
- 切诊: 脉诊 (pulse-taking at 寸口/wrist positions) and 按诊

3. Why 合参: Each method has limitations; only by combining all four can one make an accurate 辨证. The classic saying "四诊合参" emphasizes comprehensiveness. There's also the warning against relying on just one method, like "切脉而不问" being criticized.

4. Historical quotes: 《难经》says "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之之谓巧" - describing the levels of diagnostic skill.

5. Modern significance: Foundation for 辨证论治, still widely used today.
