BIA (Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, body water, etc. It works by passing a small, safe electrical current through the body and measuring the impedance (resistance) to the flow of this current. Different tissues in the body have different water content and thus different electrical conductivity. For example, muscle contains a high amount of water and electrolytes, making it a good conductor, while fat contains less water and is a poorer conductor. By measuring the impedance, BIA can estimate the proportions of various body components.
