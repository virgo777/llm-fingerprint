四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation - observing the patient's appearance, complexion, tongue, spirit, body shape, etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling - listening to the patient's voice, breathing, and smelling odors

4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation - feeling the pulse and palpating the body

合参 means combining/synthesizing all these methods together for comprehensive analysis.
