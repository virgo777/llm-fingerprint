BIA (Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis) is a method for estimating body composition. It works by passing a low-level electrical current through the body and measuring impedance (resistance). Different tissues conduct electricity differently — lean tissue (water, electrolytes) conducts well, while fat resists. From the impedance measurements, devices estimate body fat percentage, muscle mass, total body water, etc. It's non-invasive, quick, inexpensive, commonly used in body composition scales and clinical nutrition assessment.
