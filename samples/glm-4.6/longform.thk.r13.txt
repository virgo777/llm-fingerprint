四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望 (wàng) - Inspection/Observation - looking at the patient's complexion, tongue, body shape, spirit (神), etc.
2. 闻 (wén) - Listening and Smelling - listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问 (wèn) - Inquiry - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切 (qiè) - Pulse-taking and Palpation - feeling the pulse and palpating the body

四诊合参 means combining all four diagnostic methods to make a comprehensive assessment. The key principle is that a doctor should not rely on just one method but integrate information from all four to form a complete picture of the patient's condition (证候).
