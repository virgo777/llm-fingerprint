四诊合参 refers to the comprehensive diagnostic method in TCM that combines four examination methods:
1. 望诊 - observing the patient's appearance, complexion, tongue, spirit, etc.
2. 闻诊 - listening to sounds like voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle
4. 切诊 - pulse-taking and palpation

The concept originates from classical texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was further developed in 《难经》. The famous saying "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧" comes from 《难经》.

四诊合参 emphasizes that these four methods should be used together, not in isolation, because each has limitations. By combining all four, a physician can get a complete picture of the patient's condition (证候/证), which forms the basis for 辨证论治.
