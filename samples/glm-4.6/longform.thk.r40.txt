四诊  refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊  - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊  - Listening and Smelling: listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊  - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊  - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

合参  means combining/synthesizing all these methods together for comprehensive analysis.

The concept emphasizes that these four methods should not be used in isolation but must be combined and cross-verified to make an accurate diagnosis (辨证 - syndrome differentiation). This is because each method has its limitations, and only by integrating all information can a doctor fully understand the patient's condition.

This concept originates from classic texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was further developed by 张仲景 in 《伤寒杂病论》. The phrase "四诊合参" emphasizes the holistic diagnostic approach.
