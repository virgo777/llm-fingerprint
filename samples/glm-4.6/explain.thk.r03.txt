The user is asking me to explain what Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis (BIA) is in about 200 characters (Chinese). Let me provide a concise explanation in Chinese.

BIA (生物电阻抗分析) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, body water, etc. It works by passing a small, safe electrical current through the body and measuring the resistance (impedance) to the flow of this current. Different tissues in the body have different water content and thus different electrical conductivity - lean tissue (which contains more water and electrolytes) conducts electricity better than fat tissue (which contains less water). By measuring the impedance, the device can estimate body composition.
