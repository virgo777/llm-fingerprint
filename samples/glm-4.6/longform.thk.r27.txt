四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Looking - observing the patient's complexion, tongue, body shape, spirit, etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling - listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry/Asking - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation - feeling the pulse and palpating the body

四诊合参 means combining all four methods to form a comprehensive diagnosis, rather than relying on just one method. This concept comes from classic TCM texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was emphasized in 《难经》: "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧" (Looking and knowing is called divine; listening and knowing is called sage; asking and knowing is called skillful; pulse-taking and knowing is called clever).
