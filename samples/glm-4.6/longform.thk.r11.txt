四诊合参 refers to the comprehensive analysis of the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

The concept of 合参 means that these four methods should not be used in isolation but should be combined and cross-referenced to form a complete understanding of the patient's condition. This is called "四诊合参" - the comprehensive synthesis of the four examinations.

Key points to cover:
- Definition: 四诊合参 is the TCM diagnostic principle of combining the four examination methods (望、闻、问、切) to comprehensively analyze and judge a patient's condition
- The four methods briefly:
  - 望诊: observe spirit, color, form, tongue, secretions
  - 闻诊: listen to voice, breathing; smell odors
  - 问诊: ask about symptoms, history
  - 切诊: pulse diagnosis, palpation
- Why combine them: each method has limitations; relying on a single method can lead to misdiagnosis; combining them allows a complete picture

- 《素问》 mentions principles like "切脉动静而视精明，察五色，观五脏有余不足……与天地相参" 
- The purpose: 辨证论治 - to gather comprehensive information for syndrome differentiation and treatment
- Emphasize: 不能以偏概全, avoid 单独强调某一诊法

这一思想源远流长，《黄帝内经》即主张"视其外应，以知其内脏"，后世医家也反复强调"四诊合参，不可偏废"。它体现了中医整体观念，是中医认识疾病、指导治疗的重要方法论。
