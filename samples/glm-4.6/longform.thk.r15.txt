四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), color/complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

四诊合参 means combining all four diagnostic methods together to make a comprehensive analysis and diagnosis, rather than relying on just one method. This is the principle of comprehensive diagnosis in TCM.

The concept originates from classic texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was systematized in 《难经》 which states "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧" (knowing by inspection is called divine, by listening is called sage-like, by inquiry is called skillful, by pulse-taking is called clever).
