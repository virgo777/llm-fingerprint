四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors from the body
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

四诊合参 means combining and analyzing all four diagnostic methods together to make a comprehensive diagnosis, rather than relying on just one method. This is a fundamental principle in TCM diagnosis.

Key points:
- The concept originates from classical texts like 《黄帝内经》 and was systematized later
- 《难经》 states: "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧" (Knowing by inspection is called divine, by listening is called sage-like, by inquiry is called skilled, by pulse-taking is called clever)
- Each diagnostic method has its own strengths and limitations
- Only by combining all four can one obtain comprehensive information about the disease
- 张仲景 in 《伤寒论》 emphasized this principle, criticizing doctors who only take the pulse
- The famous saying "独取一诊" is criticized - relying on only one method is insufficient
- 四诊合参 is the basis for 辨证论治
- Sometimes there's 舌脉不符 cases where different methods give conflicting information - need careful analysis
- 舍脉从症 or 舍症从脉
