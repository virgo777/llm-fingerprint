四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in Traditional Chinese Medicine:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation - observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling - listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation - feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

合参 means combining/synthesizing all four methods together to make a comprehensive diagnosis, rather than relying on just one method.

The concept emphasizes:
- Each diagnostic method has its own scope and value
- No single method alone is sufficient
- The four methods must be used together, cross-verified, to form a complete picture of the patient's condition (证候)
- This is the foundation of 辨证论治

Historical origins:
- The four diagnostic methods were established by 扁鹊 (Bian Que) in the Warring States period, according to legend
- 《黄帝内经》 laid the theoretical foundation
- 《难经》 stated "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧"
- 张仲景 in 《伤寒杂病论》 systematically applied the four methods
