四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation - observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling - listening to the patient's voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation - feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body

合参 means combining and comprehensively analyzing the information from all four methods together to make a diagnosis (辨证).
