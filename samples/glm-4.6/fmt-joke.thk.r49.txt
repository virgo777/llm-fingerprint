The most classic Chinese programmer cold joke is definitely the 包子 one:

程序员的妻子对他说："去超市买一斤包子。如果看到卖西瓜的，就买一个。"
程序员回来了，手里拿着一个包子。
妻子问："怎么只买了一个包子？"
程序员说："因为我看到卖西瓜的了。"

This is the quintessential programmer joke — the programmer interpreted the conditional literally: IF (看到卖西瓜的) THEN buy ONE (item total, not one of each). It's a logic joke.
