四诊 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 - observing the patient's appearance, complexion, tongue, spirit (神), body shape, etc.
2. 闻诊 - listening to sounds (voice, breathing, coughing) and smelling odors
3. 问诊 - asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc.
4. 切诊 - feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

合参 means combining/synthesizing all these methods together for comprehensive analysis.

The concept emphasizes that no single diagnostic method alone is sufficient; doctors must integrate information from all four methods to make an accurate diagnosis (辨证). This is a core principle in TCM diagnostics.
