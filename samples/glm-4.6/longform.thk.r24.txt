四诊合参 refers to the comprehensive analysis of the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊  - Inspection/Observation
2. 闻诊  - Listening and Smelling
3. 问诊  - Inquiry
4. 切诊  - Pulse-taking and Palpation

This concept comes from classic TCM texts. The idea is that these four methods should be used together, not in isolation, to form a complete picture of the patient's condition.

望诊: Observing the patient's spirit (神), color (色), form (形态), tongue (舌象), etc. "望而知之谓之神" - one who can diagnose by observation alone is called divine/skillful.

闻诊: Listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors.

问诊: Asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. "问而知之谓之工"

切诊: Feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body. "切而知之谓之巧"

The importance of 四诊合参:
- Each method provides different information
- No single method is sufficient
- The four methods complement each other
- They should be combined to form a comprehensive understanding (辨证论治 - syndrome differentiation and treatment)

- 《难经》: "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切脉而知之谓之巧"
- 《黄帝内经》mentions these diagnostic principles

四诊合参是中医诊断学的基本原则，指医生运用望、闻、问、切四种诊察方法全面收集病情资料，并综合分析、相互参照，从而对疾病作出正确判断。

望诊：观察患者的神、色、形、态及舌象等，"望而知之谓之神"；
闻诊：听声音（呼吸、语言、咳嗽）与嗅气味；
问诊：询问病史、症状、饮食起居等，"问而知之谓之工"；
切诊：包括脉诊和按诊，通过触按脉搏与身体部位了解病情。

三、临床意义
四诊合参是辨证论治的前提。通过综合分析，辨清病因、病位、病性与邪正关系，进而确立治法方药，体现了中医整体观念与辨证思维的特色。
