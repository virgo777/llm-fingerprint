BIA (生物电阻抗分析) is a method used to estimate body composition, including body fat, muscle mass, and body water. It works by sending a small, safe electrical current through the body and measuring the impedance (resistance) to the flow of this current. Since different tissues (fat, muscle, water) conduct electricity differently, the impedance measurements can be used to estimate body composition.
