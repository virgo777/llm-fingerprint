The Four Great Classical Novels of China are:
1. 《三国演义》 - 罗贯中
2. 《水浒传》 - 施耐庵
3. 《西游记》 - 吴承恩
4. 《红楼梦》 - 曹雪芹

Some notes:
- 《水浒传》authorship is sometimes attributed to both 施耐庵 and 罗贯中, but traditionally it's attributed to 施耐庵
- 《红楼梦》- the last 40 chapters are often attributed to 高鹗 (Gao E), who completed/edited the work after Cao Xueqin's death. Modern scholarship often credits 高鹗 with continuing/completing it, though some editions list 无名氏 with 程伟元 and 高鹗 as editors. The traditional attribution is 曹雪芹 (前80回) and 高鹗 (后40回续).
