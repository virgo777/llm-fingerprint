Actually the Oct 31 == Dec 25 joke is perfect for a "冷笑话" - it's mathematically cold:

为什么程序员分不清圣诞节和万圣节?
因为 Oct 31 == Dec 25。

Explanation: Oct is octal (八进制), Dec is decimal (十进制). Octal 31 = 3×8+1 = 25 = decimal 25. So Oct 31 equals Dec 25.
