四诊合参 refers to the four diagnostic methods in TCM:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: observing the patient's spirit (神), color (色), form (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: listening to the patient's voice, breathing, and smelling odors
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Pulse-taking and Palpation: feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊)

The concept of 四诊合参 emphasizes that these four methods should be used together, not in isolation. The physician must combine all the information gathered from these four methods to make a comprehensive diagnosis (辨证 - syndrome differentiation).

- Origin: This concept dates back to ancient times. 《黄帝内经》 laid the foundation, and 扁鹊 (Bian Que) is often credited as a master of diagnosis. The term 四诊 was systematized later.
- The four methods each have their own focus and cannot replace each other
- 望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧 (from 《难经》)
