The Four Diagnostic Methods (四诊) in TCM are:
1. 望诊 (Wàng zhěn) - Inspection/Observation: Observing the patient's spirit (神), complexion (色), body shape (形态), tongue (舌象), etc.
2. 闻诊 (Wén zhěn) - Listening and Smelling: Listening to the patient's voice, breathing, coughing, and smelling odors of the body.
3. 问诊 (Wèn zhěn) - Inquiry: Asking about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, etc. (十问 - ten questions)
4. 切诊 (Qiè zhěn) - Palpation/Pulse-taking: Feeling the pulse (脉诊) and palpating the body (按诊).

四诊合参 means combining all four diagnostic methods to make a comprehensive assessment. It emphasizes that a doctor should not rely on just one method but should integrate information from all four methods to make an accurate diagnosis (辨证).

Key points about 四诊合参:
- Origins can be traced to 《黄帝内经》 and was systematically described in 《难经》: "望而知之谓之神，闻而知之谓之圣，问而知之谓之工，切而知之谓之巧" (Quote from Nanjing - the 61st difficulty)

- The principle: 诊断疾病必须四诊并用，综合分析，才能作出正确的判断
- Rationale: Each method has its own scope and limitations; disease manifestations are complex; single method may lead to incomplete or wrong conclusions (即"独取一诊"的局限性)
- 临床上不能以偏概全
- Four methods are interconnected, complementary (相互联系、互相补充、不能彼此取代)
